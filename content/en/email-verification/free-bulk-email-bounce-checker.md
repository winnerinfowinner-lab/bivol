# Free Bulk Email Bounce Checker: Audit Your Email List Health

Before launching any email marketing or outbound sales initiative, understanding the hygiene score of your customer contact database is critical. Utilizing a **free bulk email bounce checker** allows marketing operations teams to diagnose data decay and prevent costly domain blacklisting.

## 1. Why Email Lists Decay Rapidly

Email list health naturally deteriorates over time:
- **B2B Lists:** Decay at roughly **2.2% per month** due to employee turnover, company rebrands, and domain migrations.
- **B2C Lists:** Suffer from abandoned mailboxes, full storage quotas, and mistyped registrations.

Sending campaigns to stale databases results in elevated hard bounce rates, triggering automatic domain throttling by major ESPs.

## 2. Key Diagnostic Metrics Provided by a Bounce Checker

When evaluating your list through a diagnostic bounce audit, key metrics include:

```
[ Raw CSV Upload ]
       │
       ├──► Valid Deliverable (%) ──► Safe for immediate deployment
       ├──► Hard Bounce Invalid (%) ──► Must be purged immediately
       ├──► Accept-All / Catch-All (%) ──► Requires specialized hybrid review
       ├──► Disposable / Spam Trap (%) ──► High-risk infrastructure threat
       └──► Syntax Errors (%) ──► Easily fixable typos
```

## 3. How to Clean Your List Following a Bounce Audit

1. **Purge All Hard Invalids:** Permanently delete all addresses confirmed as non-existent.
2. **Correct Syntax Typos:** Fix obvious domain typos (e.g., `user@yaho.com` -> `user@yahoo.com`).
3. **Isolate Catch-All Accounts:** Segment catch-all addresses into a separate verification queue before emailing.
4. **Establish Quarterly List Scrubbing:** Audit active databases every 90 days to maintain deliverability scores above 98%.
